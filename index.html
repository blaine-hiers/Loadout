Models: text and vision models from the Hugging Face Hub (top by downloads and likes, pre-quantized repos removed), configs parsed on . Weights = params × bits ÷ 8. KV = layers × KV heads × head dim × 2 × bytes per token, with sliding-window layers capped and linear/Mamba layers holding none; MLA models store one latent per token. MoE active params are estimated from the expert config. Speed = 75% of memory bandwidth ÷ bytes read per token. Fit score = 10·log₁₀(capability × quant quality) + 1.2·log₁₀(downloads), where capability is params, or √(total × active) for MoE. Sizing one model in depth? Use Headroom.
